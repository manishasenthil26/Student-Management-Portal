About Our Portal

The Student Management Portal is a simple web-based interface developed using HTML5 and CSS.

It helps students explore courses, enter registration details and find important contact information.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide students with a simple, accessible and user-friendly online experience.

Our Objectives

  • Provide clear information to students.
  • Make student registration easy to understand.
  • Display available courses.
  • Improve access to important information.
